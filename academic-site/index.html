Research Agenda

Mechanics, intelligence and safer mobility.

Research spanning crash, crush, vibration and mechanical shock in EV battery systems, integrating nonlinear finite-element analysis with data-driven design and multi-objective optimization.

Design Optimization

Multi-objective optimization across deformation, mass, energy absorption, robustness and safety.

Current Design Study

Hierarchical optimization of multicell crash walls.

A systematic side-impact study using 15 multicell crash-wall architectures, three impact locations, three velocities and multiple wall gauges, followed by surrogate modeling, SHAP interpretation and direct FE verification.

Crash-wall architecture study

The workflow combines nonlinear FE simulations with interpretable machine learning and optimization to identify battery-pack side structures that reduce intrusion while balancing competing objectives.

450nonlinear FE simulations

0.9–1.2 mmwall-gauge range
Direct FE verification: optimized designs reduced worst-case residual bottom-shell deformation from 68.29 mm for the no-crash-wall baseline to approximately 32.43–35.00 mm.

Methods

Nonlinear FE modeling · surrogate regression · SHAP interpretation · MCTS-assisted design-space screening · four-objective metaheuristic optimization · direct FE verification.
